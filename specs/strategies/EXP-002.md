# EXP-002 preregistration — first S2 comparison (Worker 1, Q-002)

Status: PREREGISTERED. Trials executed: 0. No arm, seed, or parameter is
promoted here or anywhere in Q-002. This document is the plan; results arrive
only after the S2 baseline integrates with replay/risk review.

## Question (descriptive, not confirmatory)

Do the S2-V (volume) and S2-F (order-flow) confirmation gates change S2
pullback signal counts and reject-check mix versus pullback-alone S2?

## Arms (bounded: exactly three, params frozen per S2.md v0.1.0)

- `S2` 0.1.0 — pullback + resumption alone.
- `S2-V` 0.1.0 — plus `vol_z(60) >= 1.0`.
- `S2-F` 0.1.0 — plus 10-bar directional fraction `>= 0.58`.

No tuning inside EXP-002. Any further variant needs a new preregistration.

## Fixtures and seeds (all synthetic, `SYNTH_V1`, labelled)

- Scenarios: `s2-long-pullback`, `s2-short-pullback` (240 one-second bars
  each, `BTCUSDT`) plus `flat` and `choppy` as no-fire controls.
- Train seeds: 31, 32, 33. Validation seeds: 41, 42.
- Time split per fixture: first 70% train, next 5% embargo (excluded),
  final 25% validation (`split_indices` in
  `src/cryptobot/research/experiments.py`).

## Costs and metrics

- Costs: S2 preset (identical values to S1: `fee=5.0, spread=5.0,
  slippage=5.0, funding=1.0` bps, `conservative-simulation`); net method
  `NET_S1_V1` shared with EXP-001.
- Metrics per arm/split: `n_signals_long`, `n_signals_short`,
  `reject_counts` (per gate), `net_bps_estimate` (diagnostic only).

## Trial logging and promotion rule

- Every trial (ok or failed) is appended to `TrialLog` with
  `trial_id/exp_id/arm/seed/split/status/reason/utc/metrics`; failed trials
  are never deleted. The log ships empty; the mechanism is unit-tested.
- Promotion rule: NONE. EXP-002 is descriptive; no outcome here may promote
  an arm, seed, or parameter toward paper or live use.
