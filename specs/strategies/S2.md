# S2 range-fade (DRAFT — Worker 1)

Status: DRAFT, unvalidated, no code. Graduates only via its own spec +
preregistered experiment after S1 integrates.

Idea: fade the edges of a measured 120-bar range when momentum stalls
(30-bar |return| below a deadband) and spread is tight; invalidation just
outside the range edge, time stop 120s. Long/short evaluated separately.

Needs: range-position + stall features (Worker 1); replay bars (Worker 2);
risk sizing for range stops (Worker 3).

Bounded parameter box for the future experiment: range window {60, 120},
deadband {2, 4} bps, max spread {2, 3} bps. At most 8 combinations, fixed
before the first trial; train/validation split and NET-style labelled costs
mirror EXP-001.
