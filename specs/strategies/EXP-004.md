# EXP-004 preregistration — first S4 comparison (Worker 1, Q-004)

Status: PREREGISTERED. Trials executed: 0. No arm, seed, or parameter is
promoted here or anywhere in Q-004. This document is the plan; results arrive

## Question (descriptive, not confirmatory)

Does the S4 pierce requirement change reclaim signal counts and reject-check
mix versus the simple-reversal baseline S4-S (reclaim + momentum, no pierce)?
No hidden-liquidity claims are tested.

## Arms (bounded: exactly two, params frozen per S4.md v0.1.0)

- `S4` 0.1.0 — full reclaim signal (pierce + reclaim + momentum).
- `S4-S` 0.1.0 — simple-reversal baseline (reclaim + momentum, pierce `na`).

No tuning inside EXP-004. Any further variant needs a new preregistration.

## Fixtures and seeds (all synthetic, `SYNTH_V1`, labelled)

- Scenarios: `s4-long-reclaim`, `s4-short-reclaim` (240 one-second bars
  each, `BTCUSDT`) plus `flat` and `choppy` as no-fire controls. Primary
  fixtures persisted at `tests/fixtures/synth_s4_long.json` (seed 71) and
  `tests/fixtures/synth_s4_short.json` (seed 71).
- Train seeds: 71, 72, 73. Validation seeds: 81, 82.
- Time split per fixture: first 70% train, next 5% embargo (excluded),
  final 25% validation (`split_indices` in
  `src/cryptobot/research/experiments.py`). The split-audit embargo finding
  applies: signal mechanics only, no return-based scoring on these splits.
