## 1. Mechanism (leader-laggard catch-up)

Leader: BTCUSDT. Traded laggard: ETHUSDT. Hypothesis: a sharp
BTC impulse that ETH has not yet matched predicts short-horizon
ETH catch-up in the impulse direction large enough to exceed
executable costs.

- `BTC60` = 60-bar BTC close return in bps (see §5).
- `ETH60` = 60-bar ETH close return in bps.
- Raw gap `G = BTC60 − ETH60` (bps, SIGNED; no beta adjustment in
  the trigger — board default "raw gap until frozen", frozen here).
- LONG trigger: `BTC60 ≥ +35` AND `G ≥ +35` (same sign: BTC up,
  ETH lagging below). SHORT mirrors at `≤ −35`.

Minimum-trigger economics (honest preregistration): target = 50%
gap closure = ≥17.5bps gross; typical stop ≈ 10bps (gap-widen leg,
§6), so ≈1.75R gross. Realistic executable round-trip for small
size (≈2×5bps taker + ≈1bp spread + ≈1bp slippage ≈ 12bps) fits
inside the minimum target; the frozen conservative preset (26bps
round-trip, §3) EXCEEDS it. The edge hypothesis therefore requires
larger-than-minimum gaps, better-than-preset execution, or both —
stated here before outcomes. The frozen selection rule (§10) is the
arbiter; no post-hoc cost redefinition is allowed.

Eligible regimes: impulse-plus-lag as defined above, both symbols
on clean windows, ETH touched spread within gate. No-trade
conditions: any reject in §5.4; evaluation on any symbol other than
the laggard (`not-laggard`); unfrozen beta (`beta-unfrozen`
in the evaluation log, `beta:unfrozen` on the adapter path);
leader bar missing at the evidence instant (`leader-missing-bar`).

## 2. Bar schema (1-second replay bars, both symbols)

Each symbol's bars follow the S1 `Bar` schema exactly (`ts_utc`
tz-aware UTC on the 1s grid, OHLC, `volume ≥ 0`, `trades ≥ 0`,
`buy_frac ∈ [0,1]`, `spread_bps ≥ 0`, `source` label). Missing bars
are explicit `None` placeholders (never silently dropped). Leader
and laggard each carry positionally-aligned slot lists; cross-symbol
alignment is by TIMESTAMP (§4), never by position.

## 4. Dual-symbol feed spec (frozen)

For a laggard evaluation at index `t` with
`evidence_ts = laggard[t].ts_utc`:

1. Leader index `u` = the LARGEST index with
   `leader[u].ts_utc ≤ evidence_ts`. REQUIRE
   `leader[u].ts_utc == evidence_ts` (same grid instant);
   otherwise no-fire `leader-missing-bar` (never interpolate,
   never carry stale leader data forward).
2. All leader windows end at `u` or `u−1`; all laggard windows end
   at `t` or `t−1`. No observation with timestamp later than
   `evidence_ts` may influence the decision (causality).
3. Gap scans run on BOTH sides (§5.4); either side failing fails
   the evaluation. Halt accounting takes the max consecutive-missing
   run over both sides (`> 5` → `DataQuality.HALT`, else DEGRADED).
4. Minimum history: `t ≥ 60` AND `u ≥ 60`, else
   `history=fail` (`insufficient-history`).

Session plumbing (frozen interface, backward compatible):
`MarketView` gains optional `ref_bars` (leader aligned slots),
`ref_instrument`, and `ref_beta`; the `x1` module declares
`DUAL_REF_SYMBOL = "BTCUSDT"` and `DUAL_LAGGARD = "ETHUSDT"`;
Session populates the ref fields from the leader feed whenever it
exists (every evaluated symbol; the evaluator's symbol gate
attributes non-laggard evaluations), and passes `ref_bars`/`beta`
into `x1.evaluate_bar` for the variant path. Other families ignore
the new fields. X1 no-fires on any non-laggard symbol
(`not-laggard`).

## 5. Per-side rules (long and short evaluated separately)

Each side is an independent evaluation with its own evidence. Both
sides are evaluated for every eligible laggard bar; opposing
simultaneous fires are passed downstream (coordinator stands aside
per contract).

Definitions (all at the aligned pair `(t, u)`):
`BTC60 = (btc[u]/btc[u−60] − 1)·10⁴`,
`ETH60 = (eth[t]/eth[t−60] − 1)·10⁴`, `G = BTC60 − ETH60`.

### 5.1 LONG trigger (all must hold)

1. `BTC60 ≥ +35.0` (`+50.0` on X1-S).
2. `G ≥ +35.0` (`+50.0` on X1-S).
3. Arm gate: `X1`: none; `X1-S`: none (thresholds are the gate);
   `X1-T`: none (horizon is the variant); `X1-F`:
   `mean(buy_frac[t−9..t]) ≥ 0.58` on the LAGGARD bars.

### 5.2 SHORT trigger (mirror)

1. `BTC60 ≤ −35.0` (`−50.0` on X1-S).
2. `G ≤ −35.0` (`−50.0` on X1-S).
3. Arm gate: `X1-F`: `mean(1 − buy_frac[t−9..t]) ≥ 0.58`.

### 5.3 Arms (bounded alternatives for round 1)

- `X1` — base: raw-gap trigger, 120s horizon.
- `X1-S` — strict: 50/50 thresholds, 120s horizon.
- `X1-T` — timed: 35/35 thresholds, 60s horizon.
- `X1-F` — flow-confirmed: 35/35 + laggard flow gate, 120s horizon.

All arms share version `0.1.0`, windows, costs, and exit
construction; they differ ONLY as stated above. No other variants
exist in v0.1.0.

### 5.4 Reject checks (evaluated in order, every outcome recorded)

1. `history`: `t ≥ 60` and `u ≥ 60`, else fail
   `insufficient-history`.
2. `symbol`: evaluated symbol must be the laggard, else
   `not-laggard` (still a recorded evaluation, never an error).
3. `beta`: frozen beta present and finite positive, else
   `beta:unfrozen`.
4. `gap`: any `None` in laggard `t−60..t` or leader `u−60..u`, or
   any step `!= 1s` in either `ts_utc` window, fails. Consecutive
   missing `> 5` (max over both sides) or any timestamp disorder →
   `data_quality = halt`, else `degraded`. No intent is ever
   emitted on a gapped pair.
5. `leader`: §4 alignment (exact-ts match), else
   `leader-missing-bar`.
6. `spread`: ETH `spread_bps[t] > 3.0` fails.
7. `move`, `gapmove`, `confirm`: per §5.1/§5.2 (`confirm=na(X1)`
   on non-flow arms).
8. `bounds`: structural post-check — entry ordering, stop on the
   correct adverse side and strictly outside the entry band,
   target on the correct favorable side.

Regime labels: `leader-lag-long` / `leader-lag-short` on fire, else
`no-impulse`. Trigger strings: `btc60>=35&gap>=35` (strict:
`btc60>=50&gap>=50`) plus `+flow(10)` on X1-F; short side mirrors
as `btc60<=-35&gap<=-35` (strict `btc60<=-50&gap<=-50`);
`no-fire` otherwise.

## 6. Intent construction on fire

- `strategy` = arm id; `version = "0.1.0"`; `instrument = ETHUSDT`;
  `side` = evaluated side.
- LONG: `entry_low = eth[t]`,
  `entry_high = eth[t]·(1+2bps)`; SHORT mirrors. The ±2bps band
  with 10s expiry is the static encoding of "IOC ±2bps" (frozen
  mapping; the paper engine's matching approximates IOC economics
  for small size — labelled assumption, not venue fact).
- `entry_expiry_utc = evidence_ts + 10s`;
  `holding_limit_s = 120` (`60` on X1-T).
- Target (static 50%-gap-closure level): LONG
  `target = eth[t]·(1 + 0.5·G/10⁴)`; SHORT
  `target = eth[t]·(1 − 0.5·|G|/10⁴)`.
- Stop (static encoding of the dynamic invalidation set, computed
  with the FROZEN beta from §9): LONG levels
  `stop_btc = eth[t]·(1 + β·(−0.5·BTC60)/10⁴)` (BTC 50% retrace
  translated to ETH) and `stop_gap = eth[t]·(1 − 10/10⁴)` (gap
  widens 10bps against); `stop = max(stop_btc, stop_gap)` (nearest
  adverse level). SHORT mirrors with `min`. If either level is not
  strictly adverse to the entry band, the fire fails `bounds`.
- `exit_rule`, `forecast_cost` from §3; `data_quality = clean`
  with reason `no-gaps-N=60x2` (only clean pairs reach this step).
- `evidence_ref = record_id =
  "{strategy}:{version}:{instrument}:{side}:{iso}"` deterministic.
- The paired `DecisionRecord` carries regime, one `FeatureEvidence`
  per evaluated feature, the trigger clause, all reject-check
  outcomes, and the data-quality state. No confidence percentages.

## 9. Beta estimation step (frozen procedure)

Before any round-1 execution, run the frozen tool
(`tools/x1_beta.py`, pinned algorithm) on the frozen calibration
slice ONLY: DEV day indices 1–5, 1s closes, both symbols. Day
indices are IST days exactly as the harness resolves them
(`day_ids_for` + IST windowing; the tool shares that code path).
Method:
OLS of ETH 60s log returns on BTC 60s log returns with intercept
(`beta = Cov(eth60, btc60)/Var(btc60)`; requires ≥1000 paired
returns and `Var > 0`, else the round is BLOCKED with reason).
Record beta to 6 decimals in `beta/x1_beta.json` (with slice sha,
n, and the tool's stdout hash) and transcribe into the round TOMLs;
a frozen checker asserts TOML beta == record before any cell runs.
Beta role: stop/target translation (§6) + the §1 economics
narrative. The trigger stays RAW gap regardless of beta. No
re-estimation during or after the round.

## 10. Selection rule (frozen, mechanical)

At most ONE X1 config advances to EVAL: the config with the highest
validation REALISTIC ledger net among configs with dev net > 0 AND
val net > 0; ties break by higher dev net, then lexical config_id.
Otherwise NONE advances (`RESEARCH UNRESOLVED` unless a candidate
qualifies — reported honestly either way). The S1 control and CASH
never advance (reference only). Application is by the frozen tool
(`tools/x1_select.py`) over the frozen comparison reports; its
stdout + inputs are recorded. Position screen: report positions per
10 days per config against the 100-pos/10d screen honestly (screen
status is descriptive, not a selection override).
