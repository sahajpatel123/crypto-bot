## Question (descriptive, not confirmatory)

Do the S3-R regime filter (width cap + edge touches) and the S3-C cost cover
(entry-to-mid distance) change S3 fade signal counts and reject-check mix
versus unfiltered S3?

## Arms (bounded: exactly three, params frozen per S3.md v0.1.0)

- `S3` 0.1.0 — edge fade alone.
- `S3-R` 0.1.0 — plus regime filter (`width <= 120` bps, `>= 2` touches).
- `S3-C` 0.1.0 — plus cost cover (entry-to-mid `>= 26.0` bps).

No tuning inside EXP-003. Any further variant needs a new preregistration.
