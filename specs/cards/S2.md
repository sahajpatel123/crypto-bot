# Strategy card: S2 trend-pullback continuation v0.1.0 (Worker 1, Q-007)

Normative rules: `specs/strategies/S2.md` (D-010 hypothesis). Backtest
summary (B6); every field exact. Baseline arm for comparisons: `S2`.

## Inputs

- Causal 1 s bars, same schema as S1; explicit `None` slots for missing
  windows. No depth or flow views consumed.

## Setup

- Minimum history `t ≥ 120`. Setup (trend, swing, pullback) measured over
  bars ending at `t-1`; trigger uses bar `t`'s own fields.
- Frozen params: `TREND_N=60`, `TREND_MIN_BPS=25.0`, `PULL_M=10`,
  `PULL_MIN_BPS=8.0`, `PULL_MAX_BPS=60.0`, `RESUME_K=5`,
  `RESUME_MIN_BPS=2.0`, `SPREAD_MAX_BPS=3.0`, `ENTRY_TOL_BPS=2.0`,
  `INVAL_BUF_BPS=2.0`, `ENTRY_EXPIRY_S=15`, `HOLDING_LIMIT_S=120`,
  `HALT_GAP=5`. (`VOL_*/FLOW_*` belong to `S2-V`/`S2-F` only.)

## Trigger (all must hold)

- LONG setup: swing high `H=max(high[t-60..t-1])` first made at
  `H_idx ≤ t-11`; swing trend `(H/close[t-61]−1) ≥ +25 bps`;
  pullback low `L=min(low[t-10..t-1])`, depth `(H−L)/H` in `[8, 60]` bps.
- LONG trigger: `close[t] > max(close[t-5..t-1])` (strict) AND
  `(close[t]/close[t-5]−1) ≥ +2 bps`.
- SHORT: full mirror about the swing low.

## Rejects (in order)

`history`, `gap` (window `t-120..t`; `>5` consecutive or disorder halts),
`spread`, `sequence` (swing-extreme timing), `trend`, `pullback`,
`resume`, `momentum`, `confirm` (`na` on base), `bounds`.

## Entry bounds / expiry

- LONG: `[close[t], close[t]·(1+2bps)]`; SHORT mirrored.
- `entry_expiry_utc = evidence + 15 s`; taker-cross fill on first box
  touch after the signal bar.

## Invalidation

- LONG: `L·(1−2bps)` (pullback extreme minus buffer); SHORT: `P·(1+2bps)`
  about the pullback high. Ordering proof in S2.md §5: the resume gate
  keeps invalidation strictly outside the entry box. Stop = invalidation.

## Stop / target / time

- Stop at invalidation; target `close[t] ± 1.5R`, `R = |close[t] − inval|`;
  time exit `evidence + 120 s`; reduce-only; policy id
  `S2:stop+target+time` via `encode_exit_rule`.

## Holding range

- `HOLDING_LIMIT_S = 120` (contract range 30–300 s).

## Units

- One intent per side per bar at most; sizing owned by risk `plan_size`
  (USDT-perp, venue-quantized). Frozen `conservative-simulation` forecast
  cost attached per intent.
