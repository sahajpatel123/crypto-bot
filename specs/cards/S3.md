# Strategy card: S3 range/VWAP mean reversion v0.1.0 (Worker 1, Q-007)

Normative rules: `specs/strategies/S3.md`. Backtest summary (B6); every
field exact. Baseline arm for comparisons: `S3`.

## Inputs

- Causal 1 s bars, same schema as S1; explicit `None` slots. No depth or
  flow views consumed.

## Setup

- Minimum history `t ≥ 120`. Range/VWAP/touches/stall measured over bars
  ending at `t-1` or `t` as stated; every window ends at `t` or earlier.
- Frozen params: `RANGE_N=120`, `WIDTH_MIN_BPS=15.0`, `VWAP_N=60`,
  `STALL_M=30`, `STALL_MAX_BPS=3.0`, `EDGE_TOL_BPS=4.0`,
  `SPREAD_MAX_BPS=2.0`, `ENTRY_TOL_BPS=2.0`, `ENTRY_EXPIRY_S=15`,
  `HOLDING_LIMIT_S=120`, `HALT_GAP=5`. (`WIDTH_MAX/TOUCH_*/COST_COVER`
  belong to `S3-R`/`S3-C` only.)

## Trigger (all must hold)

- LONG fade of the low edge: range `H/L` over `t-120..t-1` with width
  `≥ 15 bps`; position `L < close[t]`, within 4 bps above `L`; stall
  `|30-bar return| ≤ 3 bps`; value `close[t] < VWAP(t-60..t-1, strict)`;
  spread `≤ 2 bps`.
- SHORT: mirror fade of the high edge (`close[t] > VWAP`, strict).
- Persistence: S3 refires EVERY bar while edge + stall + value hold (no
  novelty clause by design); single exposure owned downstream.

## Rejects (in order)

`history`, `gap` (`t-120..t`; halt/degraded), `spread`, `range` (dust),
`regime` (`na` on base), `position` (not at edge), `stall` (trending),
`vwap` (no value), `cost` (`na` on base), `bounds`.

## Entry bounds / expiry

- LONG: `[close[t]·(1−2bps), close[t]]`; SHORT mirrored.
- `entry_expiry_utc = evidence + 15 s`; taker-cross fill on first box
  touch after the signal bar.

## Invalidation

- LONG: `L·(1−2bps)`; SHORT: `H·(1+2bps)`. Stop = invalidation.

## Stop / target / time

- Stop at invalidation; **target = range mid `(H+L)/2`** (S3's
  distinguishing exit — not an R multiple); time exit `evidence + 120 s`;
  reduce-only; policy id `S3:stop+target+time` via `encode_exit_rule`.

## Holding range

- `HOLDING_LIMIT_S = 120` (contract range 30–300 s).

## Units

- One intent per side per bar at most (subject to the persistence note);
  sizing owned by risk `plan_size` (USDT-perp, venue-quantized). Frozen
  `conservative-simulation` forecast cost attached per intent.
