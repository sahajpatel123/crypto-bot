# Strategy card: S4 failed-breakout reclaim v0.1.0 (Worker 1, Q-007)

Normative rules: `specs/strategies/S4.md` (no hidden-liquidity claims:
price reclaim + momentum only). Backtest summary (B6); every field exact.
Baseline arm for comparisons: `S4`.

## Inputs

- Causal 1 s bars, same schema as S1; explicit `None` slots. No depth or
  flow views consumed.

## Setup

- Minimum history `t ≥ 120`. The reference level excludes the pierce
  lookback (pierce bars cannot absorb the level); reclaim/momentum use
  bars ending at `t`.
- Frozen params: `REF_N=114` (level over `t-120..t-7`), `PIERCE_LB=6`,
  `PIERCE_MAX_BPS=6.0`, `RECLAIM_K=5`, `MOM_M=10`, `MOM_MIN_BPS=4.0`,
  `SPREAD_MAX_BPS=3.0`, `ENTRY_TOL_BPS=2.0`, `ENTRY_EXPIRY_S=15`,
  `HOLDING_LIMIT_S=120`, `HALT_GAP=5`.

## Trigger (all must hold)

- SHORT fade of a failed upside breakout: level `H=max(high[t-120..t-7])`;
  pierce `max(high[t-6..t-1]) > H` (strict) and `≤ 6 bps` beyond;
  reclaim `close[t] < H` AND `close[t] < min(close[t-5..t-1])` (strict);
  reversal momentum `(close[t]/close[t-10]−1) ≤ −4 bps`; spread `≤ 3`.
- LONG: mirror fade of a failed downside breakdown.

## Rejects (in order)

`history`, `gap` (`t-120..t`; halt/degraded), `spread`, `pierce`
(absent/deep; `na` on `S4-S`), `reclaim`, `momentum`, `bounds`.

## Entry bounds / expiry

- SHORT: `[close[t]·(1−2bps), close[t]]`; LONG mirrored.
- `entry_expiry_utc = evidence + 15 s`; taker-cross fill on first box
  touch after the signal bar.

## Invalidation

- SHORT: `max(high[t-6..t])·(1+2bps)` (covers the trigger bar's own high,
  so ordering holds with or without a pierce); LONG mirrored about
  `min(low[t-6..t])`. Stop = invalidation.

## Stop / target / time

- Stop at invalidation; target `close[t] ± 1.5R`, `R = |close[t] − inval|`;
  time exit `evidence + 120 s`; reduce-only; policy id
  `S4:stop+target+time` via `encode_exit_rule`.

## Holding range

- `HOLDING_LIMIT_S = 120` (contract range 30–300 s).

## Units

- One intent per side per bar at most; sizing owned by risk `plan_size`
  (USDT-perp, venue-quantized). Frozen `conservative-simulation` forecast
  cost attached per intent.
