What this study does not establish

  • It does not demonstrate a false-GO rate below 1%; the N=10 upper bound is 1.9% and the N=50/100 samples are sparse.
  • It does not quantify false-GO increases for every gate-leg deletion. Boundary mutation tests kill existing generic guards; B4b lacks a PBO, month-share, n≥300, or adverse-expectancy leg.
  • The fixed 49-bps stress includes configured fees/spread only. Synthetic streams do not model market impact, funding, real execution, or liquidation.
  • Bootstrap resampling is 64 per path versus B4b production’s 1,000. Peak RSS was not measured.
