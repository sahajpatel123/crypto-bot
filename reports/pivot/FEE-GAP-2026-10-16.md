# Paper-vs-real cost gap (2026-10-16, filed for the execution lane)

Read-only audit of `src/cryptobot/adapters/paper_engine.py` (worker-owned,
NOT edited here). Two optimistic biases in simulated fills:

1. `FillModel(prob_fill_on_limit=1.0, prob_slippage=0.0)` — fills never
   slip. Real taker fills cross half the spread (~2.5bps on the frozen
   profile) plus adverse selection (~5bps/leg placeholder).
2. "Mark-quote" model: fills price off the session mark with zero
   spread. A real market order lifts the ask / hits the bid.

Net: paper economics charge ~11bps round trip (2x5 fee + 1 funding)
vs cost-contract-v1's 26bps — paper PnL is ~15bps/trade richer than
the conservative simulation the gate holds strategies to. Direction
of error favors strategies, never the house.

Consequence: a strategy that looks marginally profitable on paper
(<15bps/trade net) is expected to lose live. The fix belongs in the
execution lane: model half-spread + slippage in the paper fill path
(or haircut paper fills by the contract's adverse+spread legs) with
regression tests, then re-run economics. Until then, paper PnL must
be read with a ~15bps/trade mental discount — and the PIVOT-01 gate
correctly keeps judging on the conservative contract, not paper fills.
